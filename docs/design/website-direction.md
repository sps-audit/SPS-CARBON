# SPS-CARBON presentation website

User-authorized build, 24 October 2026. French first confirmed in chat. Presentation website and custom logo family; no operational customer account or actual carbon reporting backend in this phase.

## Direction: measurable landscape

The Atlas landscape gives the environmental subject a concrete local presence. The signature interaction reveals an ordered measurement layer over the landscape. Everything else stays precise and quiet. No fabricated customers, savings, endorsements or certification.

Palette: midnight #132D38, slate #52666B, mineral #F6F8F6, white #FFFFFF, sage #BDCCB5, olive #526642. Typeface: Manrope, self-hosted. Heading scale 42–112px desktop, 40–60px mobile. Body 16–19px. Rounded landscape 28px, interface 16px, buttons pill; do not use identical cards for every section.

Composition: light navigation / immersive landscape hero / short positioning statement / substantial interactive product canvas / method sequence / three proof principles with asymmetric emphasis / FAQ / final landscape invitation / footer. Left-aligned text except deliberate centered transitions. Detailed UI demonstration is clearly fictional.

Motion: one choreographed hero entrance and landscape/data interaction; scroll-tied orbital contour panel in the method; responsive chart/filter transitions. No constant hover-following cursor. Native smooth scrolling. Reduced-motion stops animated transformations and preserves all content and controls.

Review against generic defaults: no dark neon dashboard, no stock leaf emblem, no vanity counters or logo parade, no repeated three-card grids, no artificial scarcity. Distinctiveness comes from the landscape-to-evidence relationship, oversized quiet type and a traceable data demo.

Logo: original geometric symbol and outlined wordmark, monochrome light/dark variants, transparent SVG and PNG, print/email safe. Brand kit and gallery are real downloadable assets.

Functional contract: menu works on small screens; section links land correctly; product preview switches datasets and opens the underlying evidence; final CTA opens an accessible guided-demo modal, clearly labeled demo and no pretend signup; FAQ expands; brand files download. Demonstration figures are fictional and never claim Moroccan emission factors.

Delivery: Next.js + TypeScript + CSS + GSAP, static export, local preview, Vercel-ready source. Respect earlier Vercel preference instead of registering unrelated Sites hosting. No credentials or invented contact addresses.
