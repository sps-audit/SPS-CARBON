# Globe geometry

Coastlines are simplified from Natural Earth 1:110m land geometry distributed through world-atlas 2 countries-110m.json. Coordinates were decoded from TopoJSON and stored as longitude/latitude polylines in coastlines.json.

- Natural Earth: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ — public domain map data.
- Distribution and transformation: https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas — world-atlas, Mike Bostock; ISC code licence.
- Original downloaded file: world-110m.json, https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/world-atlas@2/countries-110m.json

This is a decorative global visual. Connections from El Jadida are illustrative and do not represent customers, traffic or measured emissions. It is not intended as a precise boundary or jurisdictional map.
